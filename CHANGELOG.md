### Added

- [21 Autonomy gap analysis](docs/21-autonomy-gap-analysis.md), [22 Autonomy architecture](docs/22-autonomy-architecture.md), [23 Autonomy development plan](docs/23-autonomy-devplan.md) and [24 Autonomy dogfood](docs/24-autonomy-dogfood.md): where the autonomous loop breaks today, the smallest coordinator that closes it, the milestones, and the campaign that would prove it
- [28 Agent lessons](docs/28-agent-lessons.md): a graded catalogue of the engineering lessons from the November orchestration and provisioning campaigns. Each lesson records what was learned, the failure or success that demonstrated it, the recommended practice, its portability, the evidence reference, its limits, and a classification of PROVEN, OBSERVED, RECOMMENDED or HISTORICAL. It covers delegation capacity, disjoint ownership and explicit-path commits, claims versus acceptance evidence, fail-closed CI and pinned dependencies, partial-work detection, durable queues and exactly-once effects, crash diagnosis, authority-bounded supersession, human-gesture boundaries, harness-agnostic adapters, premature completion claims, and the brief patterns that worked and failed. Short cross-references were added to [03](docs/03-development-and-orchestration.md), [06](docs/06-rules-and-lessons.md), [09](docs/09-reproducible-collaboration.md), [12](docs/12-three-layer-architecture.md), [16](docs/16-acdd-tdd.md), [19](docs/19-coordinator-playbook.md) and [20](docs/20-orchestrator-handoff.md). The README index now also lists chapters 25–27.
- [19 Coordinator playbook](docs/19-coordinator-playbook.md): how a coordinator agent orchestrates Kyle's rizzstack work the way MR.INDIA does. It covers roles and worker lanes, the intake loop and a brief template, the pooled bee cap and model choice, verification that doesn't trust self-reports, the ACDD/TDD gates (linked, not restated), an approval-gate table including superseded approvals, security hygiene, reporting style, memory and handoff state, the merge-plan pattern, and a copy-paste starter prompt. Kyle-specific values sit in marked tables.
- [20 Orchestrator handoff](docs/20-orchestrator-handoff.md) and the [handoff kit](templates/handoff/): how to switch coordinators mid-stream. It covers recon of the incoming host, the pack contents, approvals that carry over only as the approver's verbatim words, shared credentials now with named tokens later, plugin install with a backup, a rollback script and a post-install config diff, fail-closed automation for already-approved merges, an audit of dependence on the outgoing control surface, sandbox gaps left fail-closed, a kickoff prompt with three verification actions, the outgoing coordinator stopping new work, and a checklist.
